Privacy Policy & Data Safety

Effective Date: October 22, 2026  |  Service: KaroMix AI Studio (karomix-studio.pages.dev)

1. Microphone & Voice Audio Privacy (100% Local Processing)

KaroMix AI Studio requests Microphone permission strictly when you click Test Mic or Start Recording. All voice recording, pitch correction, EQ, reverb, cropping, and WAV mastering execute locally inside your device browser memory. Raw or mastered voice recordings are never uploaded to our servers; audio stays strictly on your own device unless you explicitly share exported files yourself.

2. Account Information We Collect

  • Optional Account Registration: Email address, display name, and hashed password stored securely in Cloudflare D1 to save your custom mix presets and subscription status across devices.
  • Local Browser Storage: Authentication token and monthly free export count stored locally via HTML5 localStorage.
  • Payments: All card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, and Google Play transactions are processed directly by PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment processors (Stripe / Lemon Squeezy / Google Play Billing). KaroMix AI never receives or stores credit card numbers.

3. Third-Party Embedded Services

Karaoke video search and embedded playback utilize YouTube IFrame Player services subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy. Public song lyrics are retrieved on demand from open lyric indexes.

4. Instant Account & Data Deletion (GDPR / Google Play Compliance)

You may permanently delete your KaroMix AI account and all saved cloud preset records at any time directly inside the app: click Sign In / Account → Delete Account & Data. Deletion is immediate and irreversible. You may also request deletion via email at support@karomix.ai.
